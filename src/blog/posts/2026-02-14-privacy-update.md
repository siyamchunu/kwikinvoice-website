Today, we're publishing our comprehensive **Privacy Policy** to provide complete transparency about how KwikInvoice handles your information.

## Key Highlights

### Your Data Stays Local
The most important thing to know: **all your invoice and client data is stored locally on your device**. We don't upload your invoices, clients, or business information to our servers.

### What We Collect
We only collect:
- **Device information** for analytics (device type, app version)
- **Usage data** to improve the app (anonymized)
- **Subscription status** (free vs. Pro)

### What We DON'T Collect
- ❌ Your invoices
- ❌ Client information
- ❌ Payment details
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Browsing history

### Optional Permissions

**Contacts Access:**
- Only if you choose to import client info
- Can be revoked anytime
- We never upload your contacts

**Storage Access:**
- Only to save PDF invoices
- Controlled by Android security

## GDPR & CCPA Compliant

For our international users, KwikInvoice is compliant with:
- **GDPR** (European Union)
- **CCPA** (California)
- **POPIA** (South Africa)

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your data (it's on your device!)
- Delete your data (uninstall the app)
- Export your data (PDF exports)
- Revoke permissions anytime

## No Data Selling

We **never** sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Period.
