We're excited to launch **KwikInvoice** — a mobile invoicing app built specifically for South African freelancers, tradespeople, and small business owners who are tired of wrestling with Word templates and handwritten invoices.

## Why KwikInvoice?

Running a small business is challenging enough without worrying about whether your invoices meet SARS requirements. That's why we built KwikInvoice to be:

- **SARS-compliant**: All 7 mandatory fields, 15% VAT support, and a pre-flight compliance checker
- **WhatsApp-ready**: Share professional PDF invoices via WhatsApp in one tap
- **Offline-first**: Works during load shedding — because your hustle shouldn't stop when the lights go out
- **Free to start**: 5 invoices per month with your business logo included

## Key Features

### SARS-Compliant Tax Invoices
Every invoice includes the mandatory fields required under VAT Act Section 20. Toggle VAT mode on for automatic 15% calculation. Our built-in checker validates 8 rules before you share.

### Share via WhatsApp
Send professional PDFs straight to your client's WhatsApp. That's how South Africa does business.

### Your Logo, Free
Add your business logo to every invoice — no paid plan required. Look professional from day one.

### Works Offline
Create invoices on a job site with no signal. Generate PDFs during Stage 6. Your data stays on your device.

### Track Payments
Keep track of which invoices are paid and which are still outstanding. Never lose sight of your cash flow.
