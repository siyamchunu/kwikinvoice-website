We're excited to launch **KwikInvoice**, a mobile invoicing app designed specifically for small businesses and freelancers in South Africa.

## Why KwikInvoice?

Running a small business is challenging enough without worrying about complex invoicing software. That's why we built KwikInvoice to be:

- **Simple**: Create professional invoices in seconds
- **Local**: Designed for South African businesses with VAT support
- **Mobile-first**: Work from anywhere, anytime
- **Affordable**: Free for up to 5 invoices per month

## Key Features

### Professional Invoices
Create beautiful, professional invoices with your business branding. Our templates are designed to meet South African invoicing standards.

### Client Management
Store and manage your client information securely on your device. Import contacts directly from your phone for quick setup.

### Track Payments
Keep track of which invoices are paid and which are still outstanding. Never lose track of your revenue again.

### PDF Export
Generate PDF invoices that you can share via email, WhatsApp, or any other method your clients prefer.
